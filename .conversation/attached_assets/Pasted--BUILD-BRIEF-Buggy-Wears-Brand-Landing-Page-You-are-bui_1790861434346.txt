## 4. The logo

The logo file is at **`src/assets/logo.svg`** (I will upload it; the original file name may be `bugless-logo.svg`, so rename it to `logo.svg`). The brand name to use everywhere is **"Buggy Wears"**.

**Structure of the SVG (already clean vector, ready to extrude):**
- `viewBox="0 0 832 1248"`.
- Group `#letter-b`, fill `#FFFFFF`: one path forming the letter **B**.
- Group `#bug`, fill `#69FF00`: two paths forming a **bug** that sits inside the B.
- The artwork occupies roughly x 192 to 681 and y 353 to 884 of the viewBox, so the logo has a lot of empty margin. Crop or compute bounds when displaying it as a 2D image so it is not tiny.

**Where the logo is used:**
1. **Loading screen:** the logo centred on black (see section 6).
2. **Hero and whole page: the 3D logo** (the main wow element, details below).
3. **Fixed navigation:** small logo, top-left, always visible.
4. **Favicon** and Apple touch icon.
5. **Footer.**
6. **Social share image** (`og-image.png`, 1200x630: the logo centred on `#0a0a0a`).

**3D logo implementation:**
- Import the SVG as a string (`import logoSvg from './assets/logo.svg?raw'`) and parse with `SVGLoader` from `three/examples/jsm/loaders/SVGLoader.js`.
- For each path, build shapes with `SVGLoader.createShapes(path)` and make an `ExtrudeGeometry`.
- White path (the B): depth about `30`, small bevel (`bevelThickness 3`, `bevelSize 2`, `bevelSegments 3`). Material: `MeshStandardMaterial`, white, `metalness 0.9`, `roughness 0.2`, `side: DoubleSide` (**polished white metal**).
- Green paths (the bug): depth about `40` so it sits slightly **in front of** the B (this avoids z-fighting). Material: `MeshStandardMaterial`, `#69ff00`, `emissive #69ff00`, `emissiveIntensity 0.35`, `metalness 0.3`, `roughness 0.35`, `side: DoubleSide`.
- Put all meshes in one group. **Centre it** using a `Box3`, scale it to about **3 world units tall**, and **flip the Y axis** (SVG y points down): put the group inside a pivot with `scale.set(s, -s, s)`.
- Lighting: use `RoomEnvironment` through `PMREMGenerator` as `scene.environment` so the metal has real reflections, plus one soft directional key light. Use `ACESFilmicToneMapping`.
- **Entrance animation:** after the loader fades, the logo scales and fades in from slightly smaller with a slow rotation settle, and the bug's emissive intensity pulses once (a soft "wake up" glow).
- **Never** recolour, stretch, outline or crop the logo, and keep clear space around it.

## 5. Scene, camera and scroll choreography

The page is one long scroll. A single **fixed full-screen canvas** (`z-index 0`) shows the 3D scene; HTML sections scroll over it (`z-index 1`). The canvas is `aria-hidden="true"`.

### 5.1 Fabric background (the cloth)
- A large, subdivided `PlaneGeometry` placed behind the logo, slightly tilted, wide enough to fill the view as the camera moves across it.
- Custom **GLSL `ShaderMaterial`**:
  - **Vertex shader:** displace vertices with slow simplex or fbm noise waves (soft folds like silk). Uniforms: `uTime`, `uPointer` (UV position of the cursor), `uPointerStrength`, `uScroll`.
  - **Normals:** recompute from finite differences of the displacement so lighting follows the folds.
  - **Fragment shader:** near-black base, soft ivory sheen on fold crests (fresnel or lambert), a very faint green rim highlight in the deepest folds. Blend `fabric-01` (the fabric photo in `src/assets/images/`) at low opacity as a woven texture. It must stay **subtle and elegant**, never bright.
- Vertex density: about 200x200 on desktop, about 90x90 on mobile.
- **Pointer interaction:** the cursor (desktop) or touch (mobile) creates a soft ripple in the cloth where the user moves, easing back to calm (spring or lerp). The ripple should feel like a hand brushing silk.

### 5.2 Camera and logo choreography (one master GSAP timeline, `scrub: 1.5`)
The user never free-orbits. Scroll alone moves the camera and logo:

| Scroll progress | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.00 to 0.12 (Hero) | Logo centred, turning very slowly. Camera at `z = 6`. |
| 0.12 to 0.35 (Craft) | Camera glides forward and skims low over the folds. Logo drifts to the **right** and rotates about 180 degrees. |
| 0.35 to 0.60 (Lookbook) | Logo fades down and moves away (opacity and scale down) so the photos lead. Fabric stays in the background. |
| 0.60 to 0.82 (Values, pinned) | Logo returns centre, **pinned** in place while three value lines swap. |
| 0.82 to 1.00 (Closing) | Camera pulls back, logo completes a turn and settles facing front. Bug glow rises (`emissiveIntensity` about 1.2). |

Add a **very subtle camera parallax** following the mouse (about 0.15 rad max, eased).

### 5.3 Post-processing (use the `postprocessing` library, desktop only)
Add these **one at a time**, testing performance after each:
1. **Bloom:** soft glow on the green bug (low intensity, luminance threshold set so only the bug and bright highlights glow).
2. **Vignette:** subtle.
3. **Film grain / noise:** very light, tiny opacity.

Disable post-processing on mobile and low-power devices.

## 9. Responsive and mobile behaviour

- Design **mobile first**, then enhance for desktop.
- On small screens: scale the 3D logo down so it fits, lower the cloth vertex count, disable post-processing, disable the custom cursor, and replace the horizontal lookbook with a vertical stack.
- No horizontal page scrolling anywhere. Respect safe areas (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`) and use `100dvh` rather than `100vh` for full-screen sections.
- Touch: the finger creates the cloth ripple. Never block normal vertical scrolling.
- Handle resize and orientation change (`camera.aspect`, renderer size, `ScrollTrigger.refresh()`).

## 10. Performance, fallbacks and accessibility

**Performance**
- Cap `devicePixelRatio` at 2. If the average FPS drops below about 40 for a few seconds, **automatically lower** the pixel ratio and/or disable post-processing.
- Pause the render loop when the tab is hidden (Page Visibility API) and when the canvas is not needed.
- Keep total page weight low (target under about 5 MB, excluding lazy-loaded images). Dispose of geometries, materials and textures that are no longer used.
- Use Lenis plus GSAP on a single shared ticker (`lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)`, `gsap.ticker.add(t => lenis.raf(t*1000))`, `gsap.ticker.lagSmoothing(0)`).

**Fallbacks**
- **No WebGL:** detect support and show the static logo image with the normal HTML sections still working.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`:** disable smooth-scroll hijacking, the cloth animation and pointer ripple, the parallax and the heavy reveals. Show the static composition and simple fades.
- Add a `<noscript>` message and basic content.

**Accessibility**
- Semantic landmarks (`header`, `main`, `section`, `footer`), one `h1`, logical heading order, a "Skip to content" link.
- Text contrast AA or better (ivory on near-black passes; do not put green body text on dark).
- Visible keyboard focus states, buttons and links reachable by keyboard.
- `aria-hidden` on the decorative canvas; descriptive `alt` text on images.
